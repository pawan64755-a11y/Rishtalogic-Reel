01

Video/Image upload

Kaise kaam karta hai

Video aur image dono ek saath select kar sakte ho, jis order mein select karo usi order mein judenge. Agar audio track attach nahi hai to har image ~5-6s dikhegi (total max 59s). Agar audio attach ho jaaye to yeh limit hat jaati hai — audio ki poori (core) length images mein barabar baant di jaati hai, aur video ko us duration ke hisaab se fast/slow (speed adjust) kar diya jaata hai, taaki audio kabhi bhi cut ya compromise na ho.

    02

    Caption — type karein ya audio se auto

    Kaise kaam karta hai

    Text khud likho, ya Hindi audio (voiceover) attach karo — Whisper us Hindi speech ko sunke Devanagari mein transcribe karta hai aur phir yahan wahi caption automatically Hinglish (Roman script) mein convert hoke aa jaata hai. Har word ka apna approx start/end time voiceover se hi milta hai, isliye highlight usi word par jaata hai jo us waqt bola ja raha hai — text edit karoge to timing dobara us Hindi audio se hi adjust ho jaati hai. Audio attach karne par woh export ki soundtrack ban jaati hai, aur attached video/images us audio ki poori length ke hisaab se adjust ho jaate hain — audio aur video/caption ekdum saath shuru aur saath khatam hote hain.

    Highlight sync 0ms
    Exact sync mein 0ms hi sahi hota hai. Phir bhi agar aapke device par highlight aage/peeche lage to slider se milaiye — right = thoda der se (delay), left = jaldi (early).
    03

    Export

    Media aur text dono chahiye export ke liye.
    Sab browser mein hi process hota hai, koi upload nahi. Export webm/mp4 mein hota hai, minimum 1080p (4:5 = 1080×1350, 9:16 = 1080×1920) aur 12 Mbps bitrate par. Auto-transcribe ek baar multilingual Whisper model (~140-190MB, Hindi audio ke liye) internet se load karta hai, phir Devanagari transcript ko yahin browser mein Hinglish (Roman) mein convert kar deta hai — Claude ke artifact preview mein sandbox ki wajah se kaam nahi karega, isliye yeh file khud host/open karke test karein.
